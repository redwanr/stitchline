Services

Five services. Each one ends with your sign-off.

The first four follow a garment from quote to floor to plan. The fifth helps product development teams see what buyers are moving towards.

01

AI pre-costing

Working prototype · denim

A merchandiser builds a quote from a tech pack in hours. That quote fixes the price of the whole order.

Our agent reads your past cost sheets, tech packs and markers. It finds your closest past styles. It drafts each cost line as a range, with the past styles it came from. Prices are masked before the AI reads a file. Each figure links back to the past style it came from.

What goes in

A new tech pack, plus your past cost sheets and markers.

What comes out

A draft cost sheet. Every line has a range and its sources.

02

SAM from video

Pilot · seeking first lines

CM, line targets and capacity are all set from SAM. It is usually estimated once, at costing. Few operations are timed.

We record a line with a phone or fixed camera. The model is built to find each operation cycle and measure its time. Your IE confirms method, rating and allowances before any SAM changes.

What goes in

Video of one line, plus your operation bulletin.

What comes out

Measured time for every operation, compared with the bulletin.

Pilot terms: one line, one product family, measured against your own bulletin.

03

Line balancing

Engagement

Measured SAM shows where the real bottleneck sits.

We reassign operators and machines around it, together with your IE and line chief. The plan is checked on the floor before it becomes the new standard.

What goes in

Measured operation times and your current line layout.

What comes out

A rebalanced line plan your team runs.

04

Planning dashboards

Prototype

One morning view, built from the plan sheets you already keep.

Planners stop stitching spreadsheets together each day. The dashboard reads your own plan data and shows where each line stands against plan. Your team owns it. There is no licence fee.

What goes in

The plan and production sheets your team already keeps.

What comes out

One shared view of the plan for the morning meeting.

05

Product trend intelligence platform

Platform in development

For PD&D and design teams. Today a designer builds the buyer trend deck by hand, every season.

AI agents read public retailer product pages and track what is new on the shelf. They draft a trend report and design concepts, each linked to the products behind it. Designers review and edit the draft before anything goes to a buyer.

What goes in

The buyers and categories your team designs for.

What comes out

A draft trend report and design concepts, with the products they came from.

We are building Bluecast, our trend intelligence platform.
