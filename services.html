The Operation Bulletin

Four service lines. Each one is scoped and priced on its own — you don't have to buy a transformation program to fix one bottleneck.

Full Bulletin — 4 Ops
01

Planning Dashboards

Plan-vs-order, buyer mix, carryover tracking — built as a single dashboard your production managers open every morning instead of five separate spreadsheets.

  • Built from your actual sewing-line plan data
  • Overview, buyer-mix, and key-insights views
  • One "number to watch" banner surfaced at the top
  • Delivered as a standalone tool your team owns — no ongoing license fee
Outcome
One daily source of truth, not five conflicting spreadsheets.
02

Bespoke AI & Agentic Solutions

Custom AI/ML and agentic tools, scoped to one capability at a time. Not a platform sale — a specific problem, solved with the smallest tool that actually solves it.

Illustrative Examples
  • Computer-vision pilots for defect or measurement detection at a specific operation
  • Demand-sensing models built from your own order and sell-through history
  • An agent that watches live line data and drafts a rebalance recommendation for review
  • An agent that triages buyer feedback and complaint data into your QA queue

How we scope it: we start with a two-to-four-week pilot against one capability, prove it against your own data, then decide together whether it's worth building into production. No year-long AI transformation contracts.

Outcome
A working pilot you can evaluate on your own data before committing further.
03

IE Automation Tools

The parts of IE work that are 80% pattern-matching and 20% judgment — stitch-type inference, seam-length estimation, thread consumption — automated with a two-layer approach: an LLM layer for the fuzzy judgment calls, a deterministic formula engine for the math that has to be exactly right every time.

  • Automated bulletin generation from garment specs
  • Thread consumption modeling (cone length, ticket number, stitch density)
  • Deterministic engine for anything safety- or costing-critical
  • Built as a tool your team runs, not a one-off report
Outcome
Bulletin turnaround: hours to minutes.
04

Line Balancing & Layout

Bottlenecks usually aren't where supervisors think they are. We treat your line as the optimization problem it is — verify SAM values against actual cycle times, quantify the real constraint, then rebalance operator allocation and machine layout around it.

  • Bottleneck and WIP analysis across the full line
  • SAM verification against measured cycle times — corrected bulletin included
  • Operator-to-operation reallocation, solved as an optimization problem
  • Machine layout adjustment recommendations, with before/after tracking
Outcome
A line balanced to its real bottleneck — target +15–25 efficiency points.
